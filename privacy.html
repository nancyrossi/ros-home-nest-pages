Privacy Policy

ROS Home Nest · Last updated: 2026-11-18

Who we are

ROS Home Nest is a personal, single-household integration operated by the developer for their own Nest thermostat. It is not offered as a commercial product to the public.

Google user data we access

With your consent through Google Partner Connection Manager, the app uses the Smart Device Management API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/sdm.service to access Nest thermostat information and controls, including:

  • Connectivity / online status
  • Ambient temperature and humidity
  • Thermostat mode, eco state, HVAC status, and fan
  • Heat / cool setpoints
  • Device, room, and structure identifiers and names

Nest cameras and doorbell video are not requested or used.

How we use the data

Data is used only to operate the household’s local ROS 2 nest_device peer: publish climate status on the local bus, and apply climate commands the household already chose to send. It is not used for advertising, analytics products, credit decisions, or resale.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the household machine (for example /data/ros-home/cfg/nest.token.json).
  • Latest thermostat status may be held in process memory and on the local ROS message bus for home automation.
  • Customer data obtained from the SDM API is not retained beyond Google’s Device Access limit of about ten trailing days, except structure, room, and device IDs and names as allowed by Google policy.

Sharing

Nest / Google user data from this integration is not sold and is not shared with third parties. It stays on the household systems that run ROS Home.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Revoke access anytime at Google Partner Connections or in your Google Account permissions.
  • Delete the local token file on the household machine and stop the Nest node to end local use of Google credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this app.
